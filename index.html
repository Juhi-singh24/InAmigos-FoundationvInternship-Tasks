About InAmigos Foundation

Registerd under Section 8 | CSR-1 Registerd | NITI Aayog Partner

Founded in 2020 by Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit organization dedicated to creating meaningful social impact across India. Guided by the belief that collective efforts can transform lives, the foundation works towards building stronger and more inclusive communities. Our initiatives focus on key areas such as education, women empowerment, environmental sustainability, animal welfare, community development, and skill enhancement. Through projects like SEVA, BACHPANSHALA, UDAAN, PRAKRITI, JEEV, and VIKAS we strive to address real-world challenges and create opportunities for growth and empowerment.
